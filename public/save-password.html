iPhone / iPad (Passwords app)

1

Open the Settings app (or the Passwords app on iOS 18 and later).

2

Tap Passwords and unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

3

Tap the + button in the top-right corner to add a new password.

4

For Website, type the address shown in the box above. Then enter the username and password you were given.

5

Tap Done. Next time you open the app, tap the password field and your phone will offer to fill it in.

Android (Chrome)

1

Open Chrome and tap the three-dot menu (⋮) → Settings.

2

Tap Google Password Manager.

3

Tap the + Add button at the bottom (or the gear icon → Add password).

4

Enter the site address from the box above, then your username and password.

5

Tap Save. Chrome will offer to fill it in next time.

Tip

Your phone usually offers to save the password by itself the first time you sign in — look for a small pop-up right after you log in. These manual steps are only needed if that pop-up didn't appear.

If you do save it manually, type the address exactly as shown in the box above, so your phone can match it to YS Connection.
